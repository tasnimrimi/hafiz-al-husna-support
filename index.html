Hafiz Al-Husna

Build a lasting connection with the Names of Allah through focused five-name levels, quizzes, daily goals, streaks, and meaningful achievements.

Learn at your pace

  • Study 100 Names, beginning with Allah and continuing through the 99 Beautiful Names.
  • Unlock each new level by demonstrating what you remember.
  • Keep learning offline and optionally sync progress across devices with Google sign-in.

Privacy and account control

Google sign-in is optional. Signed-in progress is stored in a private Supabase record protected for that account. Users can sign out or permanently delete their account and synced progress.
