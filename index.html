Islamic memorization learning app

Hafiz Al-Husna

Hafiz Al-Husna helps learners memorize Allah and the 99 Beautiful Names through focused lessons, recall challenges, daily goals, streaks, and meaningful achievements.

View Hafiz Al-Husna on Google Play Privacy policy

What Hafiz Al-Husna does

  • Study 100 Names, beginning with Allah and continuing through the 99 Beautiful Names, arranged into twenty five-name levels.
  • Complete recall challenges and earn at least four stars to unlock the next level.
  • Redo completed levels for practice without incorrectly adding repeated learning rewards.
  • Build daily streaks, earn XP, and unlock twenty Islamic-themed achievements.
  • Learn offline on one device or optionally use Google sign-in to preserve progress across devices.

Why Hafiz Al-Husna requests Google user data

Google sign-in is optional and is used only to identify the learner and connect that learner to a private saved-progress record. Google provides an account identifier, email address, and basic profile information during sign-in. Hafiz Al-Husna uses this information for authentication and progress synchronization; it does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendars, or passwords.

Signed-in learning progress—including XP, streak, learned Names, quiz results, level stars, favorites, achievements, and last activity—is stored in a private Supabase PostgreSQL record associated with that account. Users can sign out or permanently delete their account and synchronized progress.
