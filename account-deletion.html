Delete your Hafiz Al-Husna account

You can permanently delete your account and synced learning progress inside the app:

  1. Open Hafiz Al-Husna and tap the account icon.
  2. Choose Delete account and progress.
  3. Confirm Delete permanently.

If you cannot access the app, email tasnim454247734@gmail.com from the address used for your account. State that you want your Hafiz Al-Husna account deleted.

Deleting the account deletes its stored authentication record and synced learning progress. The request does not affect anonymous data saved only on a device.
