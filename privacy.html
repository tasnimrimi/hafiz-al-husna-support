Hafiz Al-Husna Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Information we process

Hafiz Al-Husna works without an account. In this mode, learning progress is stored only on your device. If you choose Continue with Google, we process your email address, account identifier, and learning progress, including XP, streak, favorites, quiz results, level stars, achievements, last activity date, and Memorize progress such as the selected Name, repetition count, and target.

The app does not access or upload photos or videos from your device. It does not collect precise location, contacts, microphone recordings, advertising ID, or payment information.

How information is used

We use this information only to sign you in, save your learning progress, sync it securely, and restore it on your devices. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising or marketing.

Service providers and data sharing

Google processes information when you choose Continue with Google. Supabase provides authentication and database hosting for signed-in accounts and synced progress. These providers process information only to deliver those services. The app never receives or stores your Google password.

Security

Signed-in data is encrypted while it is sent between the app and Supabase. Access controls are used so each signed-in learner can access only their own synced progress.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until you reset the app data or uninstall the app. Synced account and progress data is retained until you delete your account. You can permanently delete your account and synced progress from the account screen in the app, or follow the external account deletion instructions.
