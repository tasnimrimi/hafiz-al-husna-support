Hafiz Al-Husna Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Information we process

The app works without an account and stores learning progress on your device. If you continue with Google, we process your email address, account identifier, and learning progress such as XP, streak, favorites, quiz results, level stars, and last activity date.

How information is used

We use this information only to authenticate you, save your learning progress, and restore it on your devices. Google handles sign-in, and the app never receives or stores your Google password.

Service provider

Signed-in account and progress data is processed by Supabase, which provides authentication and PostgreSQL database hosting. Google processes information when you choose Continue with Google.

Deletion

You can delete your account and synced progress from the account screen in the app. You can also follow the external account deletion instructions.
